        Overview
        

        Clanker Router is a launchpad built on Clanker v5, live on Base and Robinhood Chain. It does three things:
        

          

        • Fuel (coming soon) — turn any token into AI credits for any wallet.
          • 

        • Build & Earn — launch tokens on Base or Robinhood Chain that pay their creator and, optionally, their holders on every trade.
          • 

        • Plug in (coming soon) — spend credits on [N]+ models through one OpenAI-compatible API.
          • 

        

        Your wallet is your account. There is no email sign-up.

        Fuel
        

        Send AI credits to any wallet address or ENS name. You can pay with [supported tokens] on [supported chains]. Your payment is swapped to a stablecoin and deposited to the recipient's credit account in one transaction.
        

        Credits are priced at [RATE]. The protocol fee is [FEE]. Clanker Router [never holds / briefly holds] funds during this flow.
        

        

        

        Build
        

        Launch a token on Base with a name, ticker, image and narrative. The launch goes through Clanker's v5 contracts and is signed by your own wallet — Clanker Router never holds your keys.
        

          

        • Cost: gas, plus an optional launch buy in ETH.
          • 

        • Buy and sell tax: you choose 1–10% each. Taxes go to you as the creator, minus a 15% platform fee. Clanker keeps a protocol share.
          • 

        • Holder rewards (optional): send 10–50% of the tax to everyone holding at least 10,000 tokens, paid in the pair token. This comes out of the creator's share.
          • 

        • Trading starts right away on Uniswap v4, and the token appears on clanker.world.
          • 

        • Image: upload a PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP up to 2 MB.
          • 

        

        

        

        Earn (holder rewards)
        

        When a creator turns on holder rewards at launch, part of every buy and sell tax goes into the token's reward pool on Clanker's v5 contracts. Anyone holding at least 10,000 tokens earns a share by balance, paid in the token's pair (usually ETH).
        

        Creators (and platforms that take a fee) also claim their share of the trading tax on the Earn tab: fees wait in Clanker's fee escrow until claimed.
        

        There's no staking or lockup. Open the Earn tab, paste the token address, and claim whenever you like — you only pay gas. Tokens launched without holder rewards don't pay out.
