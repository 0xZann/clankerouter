          Overview
          

          Clanker Router connects on-chain value to AI compute. It does three things:
          

            

          • Fuel — turn any token into AI credits for any wallet.
            • 

          • Build & Stake — launch tokens on Base whose trading fees are paid to stakers as AI credits.
            • 

          • Plug in — spend credits on [N]+ models through one OpenAI-compatible API.
            • 

          

          Your wallet is your account. There is no email sign-up.
          

          

          

          Quickstart
          

            

          1. Connect a wallet and get credits on the Fuel tab.
            2. 

          2. Go to Plug in, sign a message, and copy your API key.
            3. 

          3. Set two environment variables and call any model:
            4. 

          

          export OPENAI_BASE_URL="https://api.clankerouter.xyz/v1"
export OPENAI_API_KEY="cr-..."

curl $OPENAI_BASE_URL/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"auto","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"gm"}]}'
          

          

          

          Fuel
          

          Send AI credits to any wallet address or ENS name. You can pay with [supported tokens] on [supported chains]. Your payment is swapped to a stablecoin and deposited to the recipient's credit account in one transaction.
          

          Credits are priced at [RATE]. The protocol fee is [FEE]. Clanker Router [never holds / briefly holds] funds during this flow.
          

          

          

          Build
          

          Launch a token on Base with a name, ticker, image and narrative. The launch goes through Clanker's v5 contracts and is signed by your own wallet — Clanker Router never holds your keys.
          

            

          • Cost: gas, plus an optional launch buy in ETH.
            • 

          • Buy and sell tax: you choose 1–10% each. Taxes go to you as the creator, minus a 15% platform fee. Clanker keeps a protocol share.
            • 

          • Trading starts right away on Uniswap v4, and the token appears on clanker.world.
            • 

          • Image: upload a file or paste an https:// or ipfs:// link.
            • 

          

          

          

          Stake
          

          Stake a token to receive its share of trading fees as AI credits. Fees are collected [SCHEDULE], converted to stablecoin, and credited automatically — there is nothing to claim.
          

          Unstaking takes [DURATION]. Only the staking wallet can move a stake.
          

          

          

          API reference
          

          Base URL: https://api.clankerouter.xyz/v1. Authenticate with Authorization: Bearer <your key>. The API follows the OpenAI request and response format, so existing SDKs work by changing the base URL.
